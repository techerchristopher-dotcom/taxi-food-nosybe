Supprimer son compte Taxi Food

Taxi Food — application éditée par Rentanoo. Nosy Be, Madagascar. Dernière mise à jour : 6 septembre 2026.

Vous pouvez supprimer votre compte Taxi Food et les données associées à tout moment, vous-même, depuis l'application. Aucune demande, aucun délai : la suppression est immédiate.

Depuis l'application, en quatre gestes

  1. Ouvrez Taxi Food et connectez-vous.
  2. Touchez l'onglet Profil, en bas de l'écran.
  3. Descendez jusqu'à « Supprimer mon compte ».
  4. Confirmez. Le compte et les données ci-dessous sont effacés immédiatement.

Si vous n'avez plus l'application

Écrivez à techerchristopher@gmail.com en indiquant le numéro de téléphone associé au compte. Nous procédons à la suppression et vous confirmons sous 30 jours.

Aucune donnée bancaire n'est concernée, y compris si vous avez payé par carte : le numéro de carte n'est ni saisi ni conservé chez Taxi Food — il est traité par Stripe, notre prestataire de paiement, et ne nous parvient jamais. Il n'y a donc rien à effacer de ce côté chez nous.

Pour la suppression des données détenues par Stripe, c'est à Stripe qu'il faut s'adresser : il est responsable de traitement autonome pour les données de carte (voir sa politique de confidentialité).

Un cas où la suppression est refusée

Si vous êtes livreur et qu'une course est en cours, la suppression est bloquée jusqu'à la livraison : sans cela, la commande resterait en plan pour le client et le restaurant. Terminez la course, puis recommencez.

Le détail de ce que nous collectons et pourquoi figure dans notre politique de confidentialité.

Delete your Taxi Food account

Taxi Food — app published by Rentanoo. Nosy Be, Madagascar. Last updated: 6 September 2026.

You can delete your Taxi Food account and its associated data at any time, yourself, from inside the app. No request, no waiting: deletion is immediate.

From the app, in four steps

  1. Open Taxi Food and sign in.
  2. Tap the Profile tab at the bottom of the screen.
  3. Scroll down to “Delete my account”.
  4. Confirm. The account and the data listed below are erased immediately.

If you no longer have the app

Email techerchristopher@gmail.com with the phone number linked to the account. We delete it and confirm within 30 days.

What is erased, what is kept

DataWhat happens
Account and sign-in methods (email, Google, Facebook, Apple)Erased immediately
Name and phone numberErased immediately
Delivery addresses, including GPS coordinatesErased immediately

Role links (courier, restaurant staff)Erased immediately
Past ordersKept, with no link to you. Your identifier is removed: the order remains for the restaurant's accounting, with no name, phone number or address attached. This anonymised data can no longer identify you and is kept with no time limit.

No banking data is involved, including if you paid by card: the card number is neither entered nor stored at Taxi Food — it is handled by Stripe, our payment provider, and never reaches us. There is therefore nothing on our side to erase.

What we do keep about a card payment — amount, date, status and transaction reference — stays attached to the anonymised order, like the rest of the row: accounting rules and chargeback windows require it. To have data held by Stripe deleted, you must ask Stripe directly: it is an independent controller for card data (see its privacy policy).

One case where deletion is refused

If you are a courier with a delivery in progress, deletion is blocked until it is completed: otherwise the order would be stranded for both the customer and the restaurant. Finish the delivery, then try again.

What we collect and why is detailed in our privacy policy.

Eliminare l'account Taxi Food

Taxi Food — app pubblicata da Rentanoo. Nosy Be, Madagascar. Ultimo aggiornamento: 6 settembre 2026.

Puoi eliminare il tuo account Taxi Food e i dati associati in qualsiasi momento, da solo, dall'applicazione. Nessuna richiesta, nessuna attesa: l'eliminazione è immediata.

Dall'app, in quattro passaggi

  1. Apri Taxi Food e accedi.
  2. Tocca la scheda Profilo, in basso.
  3. Scorri fino a « Elimina il mio account ».
  4. Conferma. L'account e i dati elencati sotto vengono cancellati subito.

Se non hai più l'applicazione

Scrivi a techerchristopher@gmail.com indicando il numero di telefono associato all'account. Procediamo all'eliminazione e confermiamo entro 30 giorni.
