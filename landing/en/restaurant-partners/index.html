Who is behind Taxi Food?

My name is Christopher, a business developer based in Nosy Be since December 2025. I set up Rentanoo here, the platform that supports travellers from A to Z: airport transfer, accommodation, rentals, excursions — and now food.

That is where all of this came from. The travellers I welcome ask me the same thing every time: where to eat, which are the good tables in Ambatoloaka, who to trust. And residents want your cooking, but without the noise of the neighbourhood. The demand is already there. What is missing is the route between your kitchen and them.

I wanted to check before building anything: I ordered myself, here. Few restaurants deliver, and when you send a badjaj — the local tuk-tuk — the order never arrives, arrives with a bite out of it, or arrives cold. That is good for nobody — and it is your name on it, not the driver's.

Taxi Food fixes exactly that: our own couriers, the order written down in black and white, the customer pinpointed by location. You cook, we handle the rest. La Cabane was the first to trust me.
